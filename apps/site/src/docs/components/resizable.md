---
title: Resizable
description: "A horizontal split view: dividers follow the pointer, resist past limits, and snap collapsible panes closed."
component: resizable
category: advanced
tags: [resizable, split view, panes, layout]
---

The group needs a definite height. Each divider is a focusable separator: arrows resize (Shift for
bigger steps), Home and End jump to the limits, Enter hides or restores a collapsible pane, and a
double-click resets the layout.
