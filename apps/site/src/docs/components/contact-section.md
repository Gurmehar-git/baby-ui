component: contact-section
category: blocks
tags: [contact, form, support, offices, channels, section]
---

Composes `field`, `input`, `textarea`, `toggle-group`, `badge` and `button`.

`form` validates name, email and message, offers `topics` as chips, and folds into a
confirmation when `status` turns `success`. `channels` pairs a list of ways to reach you with
each one's `detail`. `offices` shows every office's local time from its IANA `timeZone`, and
whether it is open now.
