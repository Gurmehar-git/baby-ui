---
title: Expanding Search
description: "A search that waits as an icon button: it morphs into the field, results unfold beneath it, and Escape, choosing a result or leaving it empty folds it back."
component: expanding-search
category: advanced
tags: [search, combobox, morph]
---

Place it in the space the field may grow into: it fills up to `expandedWidth` of that space and
keeps the button on the `anchor` edge. Results rank title starts first and gather under `group`.
