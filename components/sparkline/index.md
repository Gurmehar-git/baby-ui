---
title: Sparkline
description: "A small trend line with a headline that draws in once seen, morphs on new data and scrubs by pointer or keys."
component: sparkline
category: charts
tags: [sparkline, trend, chart, kpi]
---

The plot fills its container's width and keeps the `width` to `height` ratio. Scrubbing swaps the
headline for the point's value and dims the line after the cursor.
