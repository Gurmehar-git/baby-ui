---
title: Marker
description: "Hand-drawn annotations that ink themselves around text as it scrolls into view."
component: marker
category: text
tags: [annotation, highlight, underline, hand-drawn]
---

Twelve styles, from a wavy underline to a box, each drawn along its path the first time
it scrolls into view. Pass `drawn` to control it yourself, for example to mark a word on
hover. Ink comes from theme tokens through `tone`; `auto` gives each style its own.
