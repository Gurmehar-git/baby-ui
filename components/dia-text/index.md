---
title: Dia Text
description: "A band of colour sweeps across and leaves the text behind it, like the Dia browser."
component: dia-text
category: text
tags: [text, gradient, sweep, reveal]
---

Text ahead of the band stays clear, so the sweep reads as a reveal. Pass several strings with
`repeat` to cycle; the width eases between strings unless `fixedWidth` holds the widest.
The band defaults to chart tokens, so it stays legible in both themes.
