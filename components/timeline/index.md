---
title: Timeline
description: "Composable horizontal or vertical milestone timeline with a snapping track, rail, ticks and dates."
component: timeline
category: advanced
tags: [timeline, milestones, history, changelog]
---

`TimelineScale` holds the active item; viewport, track, rail, item, tick, date, year and content
are parts. `orientation` is `horizontal` (snapping row) or `vertical` (date column beside a rail).
Narrow vertical scales stack the date above the content instead of squeezing either.
