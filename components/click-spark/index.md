---
title: Click Spark
description: "A burst of short lines wherever the page is pressed, drawn on one fixed canvas."
component: click-spark
category: advanced
tags: [click, spark, cursor, burst, effect, canvas, splash]
---

Mount it once near the root and every press on the page bursts. This site runs it; turn it
off under Settings, "Click sparks".

For a single area, put it inside a positioned box with `scope="parent"`: it fills the box and
hears only presses inside it. The colour comes from `tone`, so it follows light and dark.
