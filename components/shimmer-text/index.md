---
title: Shimmer Text
description: "Muted text with a bright band sweeping across it, for loading and thinking states."
component: shimmer-text
category: text
tags: [text, shimmer, loading, thinking]
---

The band width scales with the text length through `spread`, so short and long labels
shimmer at the same visual weight. Colours come from `--muted-foreground` and `--foreground`.
