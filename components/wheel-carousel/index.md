Every label sits on a circle whose apex is the selection, so the list reads like a dial. Flick
it and it keeps spinning, then settles on a label; click any visible label to turn to it.

Over the carousel the mouse wheel spins it instead of scrolling the page. Pass `scrollSpeed={0}`
where that would trap scrolling, such as a tall page with the carousel mid-way.
