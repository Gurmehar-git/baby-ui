Two stacked copies of the same word: a translucent background at a fixed scale, and a
sharp foreground copy that grows on hover. Sized in three fixed presets (`sm`/`md`/`xl`)
rather than inheriting -- this one is a deliberately large display treatment, not an
inline effect.
